Modern treatment rooms ready for patients

Jun 15, 2026Clinic update

Our treatment rooms are prepared with dental chairs, clinical lighting, and organized equipment to support consultations, cleanings, restorative care, and orthodontic visits.

Please arrive a few minutes early so the team can prepare your patient details before treatment.

Bring recent dental records when available

Jun 1, 2026Patient reminder

For braces, root canal concerns, wisdom tooth checks, implants, dentures, crowns, or bridge planning, please bring any recent x-rays, prescriptions, or previous treatment notes if you have them.

Records are helpful but not required. The dentist will still assess your concern during the visit.

Whitening and smile consultation inquiries accepted

May 15, 2026Promo

Patients interested in teeth whitening, veneers, jacket crowns, fixed bridges, or aligners may request a consultation so the dentist can review tooth condition and treatment goals.

Final treatment options and fees depend on dental assessment and clinic confirmation.

Confirm urgent concerns before visiting

May 1, 2026Important notice

For swelling, dental pain, wisdom tooth discomfort, broken restorations, or post-treatment concerns, please call the clinic first so the team can guide you on the next available visit.

For severe or emergency symptoms, seek urgent medical or dental care immediately.
